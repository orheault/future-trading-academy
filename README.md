## Purpose

This is a 24-week, evidence-driven training program for learning futures trading with an eventual focus on Micro futures. It is designed to build market knowledge, execution skill, and a documented decision process before any live-trading decision is considered.

The program does **not** promise profitability and does not treat the end of Week 24 as automatic authorization to trade live. Futures are leveraged, complex, and high risk. Progress is controlled by objective gates, not by elapsed time.

## Start here

1. Read `CURRICULUM.md` from the beginning through the Week 4 gate.
2. Read `RISK-MANAGEMENT.md` and accept the non-negotiable rules.
3. Open `PROGRESS.md` and complete the baseline assessment.
4. Complete `exercises/week-01/ASSIGNMENT.md`.
5. Record each study session in `journal/trading-journal.xlsx` on the `Study Log` sheet.

## Default operating assumptions

- Nominal workload: **8 hours per week**.
- Primary learning instrument: **MES**.
- Secondary observation instruments: **MGC** and **MCL**, introduced gradually.
- Initial trading mode: observation, then historical replay, then server-based paper trading.
- Starting live capital discussed: **USD 10,000**. This is a planning constraint, not permission to trade.
- Any future live phase begins, if authorized, with **one Micro contract** and the provisional limits in `RISK-MANAGEMENT.md`.
- The curriculum can be slowed down. It should not be accelerated by skipping evidence requirements.

## Definition of completion

The Academy is complete only when:

1. all six phase gates are passed;
2. the setup in the playbook has locked, testable rules;
3. historical and out-of-sample evidence is recorded;
4. simulation results remain acceptable after estimated costs and slippage;
5. risk-rule compliance is at least 95%;
6. operational readiness and broker due diligence are documented; and
7. Olivier makes a separate, deliberate decision about whether live trading is appropriate.

## Maintenance protocol

- `CURRICULUM.md` changes only when the learning design changes.
- `PROGRESS.md` is updated after every weekly review and gate attempt.
- `PLAYBOOK.md` changes are versioned; rules are frozen during out-of-sample testing.
- The Excel journal is the numerical source of truth.
- Major decisions are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.
