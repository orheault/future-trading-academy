## Resource-selection policy

- Official CME/CFTC/NFA/Sierra/EIA/Fed/BLS sources are mandatory where assigned.
- Books are read selectively; the goal is application, not page-count completion.
- Third-party courses may be added only when they fill a defined gap and disclose instructor identity, curriculum, costs, and risk claims.
- Avoid signal rooms, guaranteed-return claims, cherry-picked screenshots, and unverifiable hypothetical performance.
- Udemy or YouTube is supplementary, not authoritative, unless a specific course passes the review checklist.

## Third-party course review checklist

Before adding a paid course, answer:

1. Is the instructor's identity and relevant experience verifiable?
2. Are risk disclosures prominent?
3. Does the course teach method and evidence rather than signals?
4. Are performance claims independently verifiable and net of costs?
5. Is there a detailed syllabus and refund policy?
6. Does it add something not covered by primary sources and books?
7. Can the instructor or firm be checked through NFA BASIC/CFTC where applicable?

If any material answer is unclear, do not purchase yet.
