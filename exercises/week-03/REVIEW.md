## Part B, question 3 — Basis and curve, first attempt, 2026-09-27

### Instructor assessment

- Basis subtraction and sign are correct under the stated spot-minus-nearby convention. Instrument and unit are wrong: this case concerns oil, priced in dollars per barrel, not gold per troy ounce. The basis is −$1/barrel.
- Contango is the correct classification. The reason needs an explicit comparator: the deferred contract ($72) is more expensive than the nearby contract ($70). The nearby contract is also above spot ($69), but the question asked for the relationship between the two futures expiries.
- Correctly rejects a guaranteed future spot price. However, a current futures transaction price does not mean the buyer and seller share a forecast of the spot price at maturity. - Source checked: [CME — Contango and Backwardation](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/introduction-to-base-metals/what-is-contango-and-backwardation). CME discusses storage, financing and insurance as possible carry factors. Convergence near expiry does not mean today's spot price must move to today's deferred futures quote; both quotes may change.
